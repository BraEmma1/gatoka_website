Our Roadmap

A deliberate path toward stronger medical technology capabilities in Africa.

Every long-term ambition begins with a foundation.

GATOKA is currently developing its organisational structure and plans, with Togo as its proposed initial focus and Ghana and Cameroon forming part of its longer-term outlook.

Geographic Progression

Starting in Togo. Planning for wider impact.

A deliberate, phased trajectory building regional competency and technical integration across West and Central Africa.

01 Initial Focus

Togo

Establishing baseline diagnostic evaluations, calibration infrastructure, and initial apprentice training partnerships.

02 Future Expansion

Ghana

Advancing bilateral technical exchange with polytechnic faculties and specialized clinical sensor verification stations.

03 Longer-Term Ambition

Cameroon

Consolidating regional knowledge toward decentralized modular component assembly and tropicalized manufacturing alliances.

Core Priorities

Building the capabilities that matter.

Equipment Understanding

Developing comprehensive knowledge of clinical medical technologies, operational failure modes, and environmental requirements.

Engineering Expertise

Working systematically toward rigorous preventative maintenance, accredited bench testing, and hands-on technician capabilities.

Local Manufacturing

Exploring the structural foundations, component specifications, and regional partnerships needed for future medical equipment development and production.

From technical knowledge toward local production.
