Building Africa’s Future in Medical Technology

An emerging initiative working to strengthen medical equipment capabilities through engineering, technical knowledge, maintenance, and long-term local production.

Discover GATOKA
who we are 

An African initiative with a long-term vision for medical technology.

GATOKA is being developed by African medical and engineering professionals who recognise the fundamental importance of building sovereign technical capabilities across the continent. Driven by international and regional clinical experience, the initiative addresses the deep systemic vulnerabilities created by near-total reliance on foreign equipment and absent maintenance networks.

Currently in its foundational planning stage, GATOKA is establishing the operational and technical blueprints required to understand, calibrate, and sustain critical biomedical devices—beginning with preparatory workshops in Togo before scaling toward regional partnerships in West and Central Africa.

 THE CHALLENGE

Medical equipment access is only part of the challenge.

01.

Dependence on external supply

Healthcare providers across the region rely almost entirely on imported medical equipment and foreign assemblies. 

02.

Maintenance and technical support

Servicing diagnostic hardware becomes exceedingly difficult when specialist expertise, proprietary calibration firmware, and vendor replacement parts are not readily available on the continent. 

03.

Local engineering capacity

Sustainable clinical technology depends fundamentally on local people who can understand, maintain, adapt, and eventually engineer hardware domestically.

 OUR VISION & LONG-TERM AMBITION

Towards a future where Africa can build and sustain its own medical technology.

GATOKA’s long-term vision is to contribute to the development of local expertise, maintenance capability, technical knowledge, and medical equipment production.

 OUR APPROACH & METHODOLOGY

From understanding technology to building local capability.

A disciplined, staged methodology transitioning African clinical systems from passive consumers of imported technology into authoritative custodians and creators of diagnostic equipment.

01. Understand

Clinical Needs & Equipment Auditing

Engage healthcare professionals, clinical directors, and biomedical technicians across regional hospitals to systematically identify medical equipment chokepoints, recurring failure modes, and critical diagnostic needs.

02. Examine & Learn

Technical Constraints & Reverse-Engineering

Study relevant technologies, equipment schematics, tropical environmental stress factors (voltage spikes, humidity, Harmattan dust), and technical constraints under unconditioned clinical grids.

03. Develop Capability

Maintenance Knowledge & Hands-On Training

Strengthen sovereign technical knowledge, diagnostic maintenance expertise, and accredited apprenticeships paired with regional polytechnic universities and clinical teaching hospitals.

04. Build Locally

Sovereign Development & Assembly Ambition

Work methodically toward decentralized local sub-assembly, tropicalized modular power supplies, and specialized diagnostic component manufacturing over time.

OUR DEVELOPMENT JOURNEY

Starting with a focused foundation. Planning for wider impact.

GATOKA balances continental ambition with disciplined execution. Initial preparatory work is anchored in Togo to establish sound calibration and auditing protocols, with future cross-border expansion being explored toward Ghana and longer-term aspirations in Cameroon—avoiding rigid timeline claims while laying durable institutional foundations.
